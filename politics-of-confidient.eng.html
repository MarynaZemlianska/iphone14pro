Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This is a demo project for the Web{LAB} Zemlianska portfolio. The site is not a store: it sells nothing, accepts no orders or payments and is not affiliated with Apple Inc.

1. About this site

This Policy explains how iphones14pro.netlify.app (the “Site”) handles visitor information. The Site is a practice landing page built to demonstrate layout and front-end development skills. Apple and iPhone are trademarks of Apple Inc., mentioned for illustration only.

2. What data the Site collects

The Site does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The “Order” form runs in demo mode: whatever you type is checked only in your browser, is never sent anywhere and is cleared right after the check.
  • The form has no server-side handler, and the Content Security Policy forbids submitting forms to any address.
  • Please do not enter your real name or phone number — made-up values work just as well for the demo.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The Site sets no cookies, does not use localStorage for your data and loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

4. Third-party services

Two third-party services are used to run the Site. Like any web server, they automatically receive technical request data (IP address, browser type, time of request):

  • Netlify hosts the Site and keeps technical request logs to deliver pages and prevent abuse. Netlify Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the Unbounded, Onest and JetBrains Mono typefaces. Google Fonts privacy FAQ, Google Privacy Policy.

The author of the Site has no access to this technical data and does not use it.

5. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS only, loads no third-party scripts and sends security headers (Content-Security-Policy, HSTS, X-Frame-Options and others).

6. Your rights

Because the Site collects no personal data, it holds nothing about you that could be provided, corrected or deleted. Requests about hosting or font-delivery logs can be sent directly to Netlify or Google using the links above.

7. Changes to this Policy

The current version is always available on this page; the date is shown at the top.
